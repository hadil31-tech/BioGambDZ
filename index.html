About BIO GAMB DZ

BIO GAMB DZ is an innovative project dedicated to biological mosquito control using Gambusia affinis. Our mission is to provide a natural, chemical-free solution to protect communities and the environment.

Natural

Uses nature's own predator to reduce mosquito larvae.

Chemical-Free

No harmful chemicals for a safer environment and healthier life.

Cost-Effective

Affordable and sustainable solution for long-term control.

Our Pond – A Natural Habitat

Our ponds provide the ideal environment for Gambusia affinis to thrive and naturally control mosquito larvae. Clean water, aquatic plants, and a balanced ecosystem are key to success.

  • Clean and well-maintained ponds
  • Rich in aquatic vegetation
  • Safe habitat for Gambusia affinis
  • Effective mosquito larvae control
